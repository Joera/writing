# Concrete figures

## Use when
- A claim about scale or cost would be more convincing with a number (how many outlets, what a render costs, how many nodes).
- The number is known and sourced.

## Don't use when
- The number would have to be estimated without a source.
- A real case would become identifiable (case 1's money claim was removed for this reason).

## Conflicts with
None.

## Tags
`persuasion: mechanism`

## Safe or adds
**Adds.** Numbers are new claims, and they need sources ([claim-sourcing](../passes/claim-sourcing.md)).

## The move
Replace a vague quantity ("few", "cheap", "many") with the figure, its unit and its source. Round to what the source supports.

## Before
> Using the protocol has a cost — blockchain writes, distributed storage, and the network functions that render

## After
> Using the protocol has a cost: blockchain writes, distributed storage, and the network functions that render. [At current prices, publishing one article costs roughly N, source.]

*(Placeholder: no figure is in the source.)*

## Pitfalls
- False precision.
- Figures that date quickly without a date attached.
