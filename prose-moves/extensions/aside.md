# Aside

## Use when
- The writer is visible, and a short personal remark, admission of bias, or tangent builds trust or clarity.
- Essays and posts.

## Don't use when
- Presence is invisible (ruled out for the Polyphony white paper).
- The aside is longer than the point it interrupts.

## Conflicts with
- `presence: invisible`; `register: plain` in formal documents.

## Tags
`presence: visible`, `register: colloquial` (also Palmer's elevated version).

## Safe or adds
**Adds.** An aside is new material.

## The move
Step out of the argument for one or two sentences, mark the step (brackets, "an aside:", or a dash), say the thing, and step back in.

## Before
> Polyphony is a protocol, not a platform

## After (in an essay)
> Polyphony is a protocol, not a platform. (I have spent enough years on other people's platforms to be suspicious of new ones, my own included.)

## Pitfalls
- Asides that are the most interesting thing in the paragraph.
- Asides as a habit.
