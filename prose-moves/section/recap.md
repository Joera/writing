# Recap

## Use when
- A long technical stretch has ended and the reader needs to know what they now know before the next part.
- A coined term has just been earned and should be fixed in memory.

## Don't use when
- The document setting forbids restatement ("each formulation stated once in full; one deliberate repetition"). Then use the recap as that one repetition, or not at all.
- The section is short.

## Conflicts with
- [spiral-return](spiral-return.md) for the same return; [return-to-cases](../document/return-to-cases.md) as the closing.

## Tags
`register: plain` (also colloquial).

## Safe or adds
Safe. A recap that says anything the section did not is an add.

## The move
In two or three sentences, say what the section established, in the terms the reader will need next. Use the coined terms. No new claims.

## Before
> [Section 6 ends on governance thresholds.]

## After
> So: only approved code, running with the approved key, can produce a page the record points at. Governance can stop a page from being rendered. It cannot make the machinery render one that was not agreed to.

## Pitfalls
- Recapping after every section.
- A recap that drifts from the wording it summarises.
