# Sentence pass

## What it checks
The sentence level, done once over a finished section rather than chosen per paragraph. In practice the sentence work that matters is diction, terms and accuracy; rhythm matters mostly at closes.

## When to run
After a section's paragraphs are settled, before the whole-text passes.

## How
1. **Diction.** Every word a smart but not bookish reader would stop at: replace it or explain it in the same breath ("disposition" → "habit"). See [plain-diction](../sentence/plain-diction.md).
2. **Terms.** Every key term in its glossary form ("pseudonymous", not "a pseudonym"; "renderer", not "generator"). See [term-discipline](../sentence/term-discipline.md).
3. **Referents.** Every "this", "they", "the neighbors" points at something the reader can find.
4. **Accuracy.** Every small qualifier is true ("for anyone" when only authorised parties can act). Flag doubts for the [author fact check](author-fact-check.md).
5. **Rhythm against the settings.** Check the document's rhythm setting, sentence by sentence only where it matters: runs of same-length sentences, a periodic sentence outside a gate condition. At section closes, apply [cadence](../sentence/cadence.md).
6. **Echoes.** Where a phrase from earlier can be picked up deliberately ("delay, argue or refuse"), keep it; where a repetition is accidental, cut it.

The files in [sentence/](../sentence/) are this pass's reference, not separate choices in the plan.

## Output
A list per paragraph: location · issue · fix.
