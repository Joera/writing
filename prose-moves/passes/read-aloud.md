# Read aloud

## What it checks
*How does it sound?* Finds sentences that trip, run out of breath, bury their stress, or repeat a sound or rhythm by accident.

## When to run
After rewriting. Always on the introduction and on section closes.

## How
1. Read the passage aloud, at speaking pace, once without stopping.
2. Mark every place you stumbled, ran out of breath, or had to re-read.
3. Mark sentences where the stressed word is not near the end.
4. Mark accidental rhymes, repeated sentence openings, and runs of same-length sentences.
5. Fix by reordering or splitting, never by changing meaning.

## Output
Marked passages with a one-line note each.

## Example
- §1 P7: "and since a replacement can add new work but can never restore what the name once vouched for, that corpus is a loss no contributor can refill" → out of breath; split after "vouched for".

## Source
Le Guin's lens: sound first (*Steering the Craft* opens on the sound of prose). Adopted in the Polyphony document choices as "read-aloud pass on the introduction".
