# Cumulative sentence

## Use when
- A main clause needs several details attached, and the reader should get the point first and the detail after.
- Describing a situation, a scene, or the parts of a system.

## Don't use when
- The detail must be understood before the point (use [periodic-sentence](periodic-sentence.md)).
- The paragraph is periodic in rhythm.

## Conflicts with
- [periodic-sentence](periodic-sentence.md) as the dominant shape of the same paragraph.

## Tags
`rhythm: paratactic`

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
State the main clause first, complete. Then add modifiers after it, each one a phrase that narrows or extends what came before. Commas, not subordinate clauses.

## Before
> Two journalists work a beat together for years — same sources, same enemies, a running series of investigations that readers cite by the site's name rather than either byline.

## After
> Two journalists work a beat together for years: the same sources, the same enemies, a running series of investigations that readers cite by the site's name rather than by either byline.

## Pitfalls
- Adding modifiers until the sentence loses its subject.
- Every sentence cumulative, so nothing lands.

[build-then-land](../paragraph/build-then-land.md), [catalogue-paragraph](../paragraph/catalogue-paragraph.md)
