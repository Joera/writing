# Hinge connectives

## Use when
- The logic between sentences is real but unmarked, so the reader has to infer "because" or "so".
- Or it is marked with heavy connectives ("furthermore", "consequently", "it should be noted that").

## Don't use when
- The logic is obvious from order alone; a connective would over-explain.

## Conflicts with
- `register: elevated` (use the elevated equivalents instead).

## Tags
`register: plain` (also colloquial).

## Safe or adds
Safe, provided the connective names logic the source actually has. A "so" that asserts a causation the source didn't claim is an add.

## The move
Mark turns with short conversational connectives at the start of a sentence: *So*, *But*, *And*, *Still*, *Which is why*, *It works like this.*, *So consider…*. One per sentence, at most one every few sentences.

## Before
> The party with less to lose sets the terms, and everyone inside adjusts without being asked to.

## After
> So the party with less to lose sets the terms, and everyone inside adjusts without being asked to.

## Pitfalls
- "So" at the start of every paragraph.
- A connective that claims a causal link the text does not support.
