# Question, then answer

## Use when
- A paragraph answers a question the reader has but the text never states.
- Opening a section whose job is to answer one question.

## Don't use when
- More than once a section.
- The question is rhetorical and the answer is obvious.

## Conflicts with
None.

## Tags
None. In an impersonal document, phrase the question impersonally ("Who can set the generator in motion?" not "You might ask…").

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
Ask the question in one short sentence. Answer it in the next, directly, first words first.

## Before
> The first gate is the right to rerender the publication. It is open to the author of a work, an owner of the publication, or someone the publication has authorized

## After
> Who may set the generator in motion? Only the author of a work, a holder of the publication, or someone the publication has authorized. That is the first gate.

## Pitfalls
- A question the text then doesn't answer.
- Questions as a substitute for topic sentences throughout.
