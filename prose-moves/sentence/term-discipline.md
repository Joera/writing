# Term discipline

## Use when
- Always, once a term is defined or coined. This move is less a choice than a rule.

## Don't use when
- Narrative passages where the glossary allows an everyday word ("writer" for author, "magazine" for publication, in cases only).

## Conflicts with
- Elegant variation (swapping synonyms to avoid repetition).

## Tags
None.

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
Use the defined term every time, in the same form. Do not vary it for style. If the text needs two words, define two terms. When a term replaces another, replace every instance.

## Before
> It is open to the author of a work, an owner of the publication, or someone the publication has authorized [section 5]
> set in motion by an author, a publication owner, or someone the publication has authorized [section 6]

## After
> It is open to the author of a work, a holder of the publication, or someone the publication has authorized. [both places]

## Pitfalls
- Fixing one instance and missing others: run [term-consistency](../passes/term-consistency.md).
- Repeating a term so often that a pronoun would be clearer. Pronouns are fine; synonyms are not.
