# Balanced pair

## Use when
- A sentence states two things that mirror each other (author and publication; leave and stay), and the mirroring is the point.

## Don't use when
- The two halves are not truly parallel (case 1's two accounts are not true opposites).
- The pair would read as an epigram the reader must decode.

## Conflicts with
- [triad](triad.md) in the same sentence.

## Tags
None.

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
Put the two halves in the same grammatical shape, same length, same verb pattern, separated by a semicolon or a full stop.

## Before
> An author appends a work and can retract it; a publication admits a work and can decline to keep carrying it.

## After
> An author adds a work and can withdraw it. A publication admits a work and can stop carrying it.

## Pitfalls
- Forcing symmetry onto asymmetric facts.
- Antithesis as a tic ("not X but Y" in every paragraph).
