# Periodic sentence

## Use when
- A condition must be understood before the result can be (gate conditions: "only when X, and only while Y, may Z").
- The point should arrive at the end, with weight.

## Don't use when
- The document is paratactic and this is not a gate condition. The Polyphony setting allows periodic sentences "only for gate conditions".

## Conflicts with
- [cumulative-sentence](cumulative-sentence.md) as the dominant shape of the same paragraph.

## Tags
`rhythm: periodic`

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
Put the conditions, qualifications and subordinate clauses first, and hold the main clause back until the end. The sentence is not grammatically complete until its last words.

## Before
> Both gates must pass before the generator may use a work's source material.

## After
> Only when the caller has the right to rerender, and only while the work is under a current agreement, may the generator use its source material.

## Pitfalls
- Holding back so long the reader loses the thread.
- Using it where nothing needs holding back.

## Combines with
[qualifying-parenthesis](../paragraph/qualifying-parenthesis.md), [hinge-connectives](hinge-connectives.md)

## Source
Palmer (*Terra Ignota*'s eighteenth-century periodic sentences).
