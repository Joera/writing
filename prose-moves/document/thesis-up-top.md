# Thesis up top

Variant: **promise the payoff** (below).

## Use when
- Readers will skim, or stop after the first paragraph (abstracts, summaries, posts).
- The argument can be stated in two or three plain sentences.
- The rest of the text is unpacking, not discovery.

## Don't use when
- The thesis only makes sense after the reader has seen cases: use [cases-first](cases-first.md).
- The text holds a question open on purpose (certainty: open).

## Conflicts with
- [cases-first](cases-first.md), [thought-experiment](thought-experiment.md) and [name-the-picture-first](name-the-picture-first.md) for the same opening.

## Tags
`certainty: flat` (flat, with limits, or flat, polemical). A calibrated text can use the variant.

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
State the whole argument at the start: the problem, the cause, the remedy. Use the plainest words available; save the mechanism for later. Three plain sentences beat one clever one. Then unpack in the order the thesis named things.

**Promise the payoff (variant).** Instead of stating the conclusion, say what the reader will have by the end ("By the end of this section it should be clear why ending an agreement is a fact rather than a request"). Use when the conclusion needs the setup to be believed.

## Before
> Publishing together runs on dependency. The writer's byline, income, and audience sit in whatever system the publication or the platform controls, so the side that can afford to walk away sets the terms. This paper proposes a publishing protocol in which neither party holds that position: [...]

## After
> Writers have only a handful of places to publish, and leaving one usually means leaving their work behind, so it pays to agree. Polyphony fixes both: writers keep their work when they leave, and anyone can start a publication cheaply, alone or with others. When publications have to compete for writers, disagreement has somewhere to go, and the media stops speaking in unison.

## Pitfalls
- An epigram instead of a thesis. "The unison is not enforced. It is priced." is memorable and cryptic; run [epigram-audit](../passes/epigram-audit.md).
- Putting the mechanism in the thesis. Say the good news; leave how it works for the unpacking.
- Promising a payoff the text never delivers.
