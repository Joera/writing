# Plain close

## Use when
- A paragraph or section ends on an ornate or abstract sentence, and the real point is simpler.
- The last sentence currently tries to be memorable.

## Don't use when
- The close is the document's one deliberate repetition or cadence (then use [cadence](../sentence/cadence.md)).

## Conflicts with
- [qualifying-parenthesis](qualifying-parenthesis.md) in the closing sentence.

## Tags
`register: plain`

## Safe or adds
Safe.

## The move
End the paragraph with the plainest true sentence available: subject, verb, object, short words. If the paragraph's point can be said in eight words, end with those eight.

## Before
> Coordination stays in human hands; the leverage passes to machinery with no interests. Editorial judgment survives. What goes is the leverage, which was never the craft.

## After
> People still decide what to publish. The machinery only removes the leverage.

*(A cut: check with [fidelity-check](../passes/fidelity-check.md) that "which was never the craft" is not a claim the author needs.)*

## Pitfalls
- Flattening a close that was doing real work.
- Every paragraph ending the same way.
